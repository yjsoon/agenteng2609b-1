Three guiding principles

From Module 1.

  1. Focus on the problem. Diagnose the real problem first, then decide what to build.
  2. Chase outcomes. Success is the change you cause, not the features you ship.
  3. Integrate policy, ops, tech. The fix may be in any of the three.

Spotting a solution in disguise · Lesson 1

Solution in disguiseNames an artefact: app, chatbot, dashboard, “use AI to…”. Often phrased as “we need a…”.
ProblemNames who is struggling, what goes wrong, and what it costs. No fix in it.

Unwrap check: specific who · what goes wrong today · cost · no artefact.
