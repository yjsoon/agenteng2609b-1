# Mission: Product Thinking (IDG's AI Build 301 pathway)

## Why
Stop building the wrong thing. Use product thinking to scope my own projects, so that before I build anything I can pin down the real problem, who has it, and how I'd know it's solved.

## Success looks like
- Given a project idea, I can tell when it's a solution in disguise and restate it as a problem
- I can run the Five Whys on a real problem and choose a root cause my team can actually act on
- I can write a problem statement using IDG's 4Cs (Clarity, Consequence, Cause, Confirmation)
- I can pick an outcome metric (not an output) plus a leading indicator for a project
- I can name the riskiest assumption in a project and the cheapest way to test it before building

## Constraints
- About 10 minutes per session: one short lesson and a quiz
- No running project yet: use realistic case studies until I have one (then switch to it)
- Ground teaching in the Institute of Digital Government's product thinking pathway

## Out of scope
- Product management as a career (roadmaps, stakeholder politics, hiring)
- Deep UX research methods and visual design
